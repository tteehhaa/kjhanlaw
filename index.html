01 / ABOUT

Kyeongjae Han,
Attorney at Law.

I am an attorney admitted in the Republic of Korea. I passed the 53rd Korean National Bar Examination and completed the 43rd class of the Judicial Research and Training Institute, and have since handled civil, criminal, and administrative matters alongside advisory work.

I also serve as court-appointed defense counsel for the Seoul Metropolitan Government and the Supreme Court of Korea, and as a member of the Korean Bar Association Startup Legal Support Group.

Every matter rests on its own facts. Before arguing the issues, I set out the facts and how the law applies to them, then explain the options that are actually open to you. My work also covers matters involving cross-border transactions and investment.

If the facts are hard to organize, bring them as they are. Sorting them out is where we start.

Kyeongjae Han
